# Kid UI guidelines (age 4, cannot read)

Full rationale: `docs/07-kid-gui-design.md`.

1. **Pictures first.** The child-facing UI must work with no text: quantities are dots (●●●), angles are arc lengths, states are glows/poses, directions are arrows. Text exists as (a) `title` tooltips and short `data-label` captions that only render when the grown-up "helper words" toggle is on (`body.labels`), (b) the parent guide overlay (`#guide`, built from each piece's `label`/`help`), (c) the gear panel. Every new piece needs `label(p)` and `help`.
2. **Jigsaw pieces.** Program pieces are rendered by `pieceSvg()` in `editor.js` with `puzzlePath(order, first, last)` (a special case of `framePath(order, W, H, first, last)`): round hole on the leading edge, mushroom knob (36 px) on the trailing edge, 124 px body (top-to-bottom pieces are `HOLE_DEPTH` taller and their picture starts below the hole so connectors never overlap the icon), 4 px dark outline in the family colour, white inner highlight. Neighbours overlap by the knob size (`--knob` in CSS) so knobs sit in holes. The last piece of a strip or of a repeat has no knob. A repeat is ONE big piece: `.piece-container` holds a `.container-frame` SVG (path drawn after layout by `_drawFrames()` from the measured size, with an inner knob for the first child), the head picture and the `.container-body`. Repeats are never nested (`_dropTarget` refuses). Left-to-right rows wrap; `_drawWrapArrows()` draws the dotted snake arrow in `.wrap-overlay`. The strip is zoomed with CSS `zoom` (`Editor.zoomBy`). Inside a piece `pieceInner()` lays out three fixed rows (`.piece-dots` 16 px, `.piece-icon` 64 px square, `.piece-words`) that keep their size whether helper words are on or off, so pictures are always the same size, centred, and clear of the hole on the left; counts come from the piece's `dots(p)` and are drawn by `Icons.dots(n)`, never inside the picture; every piece with `params` has dots (position in the ascending tap order), pieces without settings have none. Don't introduce rectangles for program steps, and never let overlays (robot window, panels) cover the strip.
3. **Colour families are fixed** (`Groups` in `pieces.js`): move blue `#4da3ff`, turn purple `#7b61ff`, light yellow `#ffd166`, magnet pink `#ef476f`, wait/sense orange `#ff9f43`, repeat green `#2ecc40`. New family = new colour + docs entry.
4. **Reading direction toggle** must keep working: anything that lays out pieces checks `program.order` ('ltr' | 'ttb') and the strip's `data-order` CSS.
5. **Touch targets ≥ 74 px**, pieces 104 px, pad buttons 120 px; `touch-action: none` on draggable areas; drags start after 8 px of movement so taps still cycle parameters; holding 0.5 s opens the − / + stepper popover (`#popover`). Palette size/layout/sort are user settings (`Editor.settings`, persisted) — new palette features must work in list, grid and tray layouts at all three sizes.
6. **Feedback for every action**: tap blip, running piece wiggles/glows with a tick, success chime, refusal buzz (`sound.js`), trash grows while dragging, shakes on a tap and tips over on a triple tap (which clears the program). Never fail silently.
7. **Predictable drops**: a drop joins the program only within ~50 px (× zoom) of an existing piece or the start flag (`_dropTarget`); anywhere else the piece is discarded with the trash sound. Exceptions: the end of the program has a bigger landing zone (`END_ZONE` = 180 px past the last top-level piece in the reading direction), and an empty program accepts a drop anywhere inside the editor window (`stripWrapEl`), otherwise the first piece could only land on the tiny start flag. Rows/columns use `align-items: center` so connectors of tall pieces (repeat) stay on the shared midline. Play without a robot → flash the Bluetooth button and buzz; Stop always available and always works (also from the Drive tab).
8. **Sensor pictures, not values** (Drive tab): bumper squashes and glows, eye iris takes the colour, battery fill shrinks and changes colour, compass needle turns, crash star flashes. Thresholds come from VEXcode (`hueToColor`, proximity ≥ 50).
9. **Grown-up things go behind the gear**: connection mode, logs, clear, hints. Keep that panel out of the child's reach visually (small, low-contrast button).
10. **Challenge boards are pictures too** (`drawBoard` in challenge.js): brick walls, pale floor, gold coins, big star, coloured tiles/pucks/boxes, striped doors with a red/green lamp, a faint start arrow; progress changes the picture in place with a sound; a win is a star-grade card (1–3 stars) with a next arrow, no words. Level cards are thumbnails of the same drawing. See the `challenge-design` skill.
11. **Children are pictures**: the current child is an animal avatar button; switching and adding a child is a picture picker; removal is a grown-up panel action.
12. **Verify on a touch screen size** (`@media (max-width: 800px)` rules) and in both reading directions before calling a UI change done.
